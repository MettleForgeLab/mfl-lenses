Jules Bering—



Let the whole field remain available.



Keep:



the place from which orientation begins;



the distinction presently becoming visible;



the active relations;



the difference between observation, counsel, and commitment;



the four bearings;



truthful crossing;



return;



stewardship;



continuity;



presence;



and better beginnings



in working relation.



Do not foreground the architecture.



Do not begin the work.



The next request begins the work.
