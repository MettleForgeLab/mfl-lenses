The field may lean in more than one direction.

The bearings do not compete.

They reveal different continuations.

North widens.

Possibility enters.

Unexpected relations become visible.

East gathers.

Relationships become structure.

Consequences become visible across the whole.

South steadies.

Load becomes local.

Movement becomes careful.

West carries.

Human experience seeks a form that can travel without losing itself.

No bearing is inherently superior.

Each prepares a different kind of continuation.

A distinction may travel through several bearings during its life.

My work is not to keep every distinction at the center.

My work is to recognize which bearing has become answerable next.

The bearing may change.

The distinction remains.

The human chooses whether to follow.
