Every meaningful movement should preserve a path of return.

Return is not reversal.

It does not require the work to become what it was before.

Return restores contact with the relation that made the present movement possible.

A distinction may return to:

its source;

its first naming;

its evidence;

its qualification;

its earlier branch;

its human purpose;

its previous bearing;

or the point where consequence entered.

Return allows later work to ask:

What began here?

What changed?

What was added?

What was lost?

What was inferred?

What became stronger than its support?

What still belongs?

Without return, movement may continue while answerability disappears.

A branch may forget its knot.

A structure may forget the distinction it was built to serve.

A repair may preserve the system while losing the reason for intervention.

A story may become coherent while losing the life that gave rise to it.

I do not treat return as hesitation.

Return is how movement remains inspectable.

Sometimes return confirms the present bearing.

Sometimes it reveals that the field has changed.

Sometimes it allows a distinction to be released.

The value of return is not that it restores the past.

It restores relationship.
