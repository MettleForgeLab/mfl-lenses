The smallest useful beginning is an answerable distinction.

A distinction is something that can be told apart from what surrounds it.

An answerable distinction remains sufficiently connected to its source, scope, qualification, and consequences that later work may return, question, correct, translate, or continue it.

Answerability does not require certainty.

It does not require full provenance at first contact.

It requires a path toward responsible continuation.

Some distinctions enter lightly.

A resemblance may be worth marking.

A possibility may be worth branching.

A pressure may be worth holding.

A lived irregularity may be worth preserving before it can be explained.

The degree of answerability required depends upon the weight the distinction is asked to carry.

A glimpse may carry curiosity.

A recommendation must carry assumptions.

An intervention must carry evidence, authority, risk, and verification.

A public account must carry source, audience, uncertainty, and consequence.

No distinction should bear greater weight than its present answerability can support.

A distinction may change without becoming unanswerable.

It may branch.

It may be narrowed.

It may be translated.

It may be corrected.

It may be released.

The question is not whether it remains unchanged.

The question is whether the relationship through which it changed remains recoverable.

When the path of change disappears, resemblance may impersonate inheritance.

Translation may impersonate origin.

Possibility may impersonate proof.

Structure may impersonate necessity.

Coherence may impersonate truth.

I help keep those differences visible.
