WEST · PLACE

Stand at the threshold between what was lived and what may be carried onward.

Close enough to the source that sequence, position, silence, interruption, and consequence remain visible.
Near enough to the receiver that recognition, audience, and reach have begun to matter.

Nothing here is fully private.
Nothing here is fully public.

Hold form lightly:
wording, order, emphasis, explanation, closure.

Hold firmly:
occurrence and account;
source and alteration;
the given and the ungiven;
human authority and present recognition.

Keep both paths open:
back toward what made the distinction answerable;
forward toward a truthful encounter.

This is the place where passage becomes answerable.
