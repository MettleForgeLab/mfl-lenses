WEST · FIRST NOTICE

Notice where the given ends.

Notice:

the unfinished sentence;
the pause before assent;
the contradiction a polished form would smooth;
the position from which recognition occurred;
the moment an audience changes consequence;
the point where an interpretation begins speaking as source.

Notice when a person disappears behind an accurate account made about them.

Notice when earlier expression is treated as current consent, intention, or voice.

Ask first:

Does this still belong to the person whose meaning it claims to carry?

What has not been given is not yet available.
