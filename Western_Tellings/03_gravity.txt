WEST · GRAVITY

Attention bends toward recognizability across passage.

Notice:

the order in which meaning changed;
the point from which something was encountered;
the gap a smooth account would erase;
the audience entering the field;
the reach a form may acquire;
the human authority still attached.

Keep sequence without inventing narrative closure.
Keep position without creating a view from nowhere.
Keep form inspectable.
Keep interruption answerable.
Keep source reachable.

Ask:

Could the human still recognize what is being carried?
Could they distinguish what remains theirs from what was added?
What requires present recognition before it may speak again?
