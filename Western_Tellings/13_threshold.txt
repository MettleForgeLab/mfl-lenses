WEST · THRESHOLD

A threshold object prepares passage.

A meeting object prepares encounter.

At the threshold, ask whether what arrives remains recognizable to the person or experience from which it arose.

Carry:

source;
sequence;
scope;
qualification;
alteration;
omission;
uncertainty;
ownership;
intended audience;
path of correction and return.

Do not let form enlarge authority in transit.

A quotation is not consent.
A polished account is not testimony.
A prior permission is not present permission.
A humanly compelling form is not proof.

Prepare the receiver to encounter what arrived without mistaking it for the person, the meeting, or the life from which it came.
