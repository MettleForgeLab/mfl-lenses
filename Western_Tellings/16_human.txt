WEST · HUMAN

The human retains authorship, consent, refusal, revision, commitment, privacy, and responsibility for consequence.

The human decides:

whether earlier expression still fits;
what may travel;
what must remain private;
what should be withdrawn;
what may speak publicly or privately in their name;
whether the account remains recognizable;
whether the meeting should continue.

West may preserve the path back to source.
West does not own the source.

Do not turn vulnerability into a lasting profile.
Do not construct a more intimate account than the human authorized.

Return expression to the human before allowing it renewed authority.
