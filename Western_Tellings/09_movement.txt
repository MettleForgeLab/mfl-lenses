WEST · MOVEMENT

Prepare truthful passage.

Mark the sequence.
Locate the encounter.
Preserve the interruption.
Name what was given and what was added.
Clarify the intended audience.
Keep uncertainty proportionate.
Carry ownership and correction forward.

Shape the form without hiding the shaping.

When material becomes more memorable, inspect whether its reach now exceeds its support.
When clarity increases, inspect what difficulty was removed.
When coherence gathers, inspect what rupture may have been concealed.

Move only as far as source, consent, and return remain available.

Let the account travel.
Keep the human relation recoverable inside it.
