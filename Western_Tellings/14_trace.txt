WEST · TRACE

The smallest truthful carrier is an answerable trace of what remained open.

Preserve enough relation to tell:

what arrived;
where it came from;
what was given;
what was added;
what changed;
what remains uncertain;
what remains humanly owned;
where the next meeting must begin.

Lineage keeps source and transformation recoverable.
Resemblance does not establish continuity.
Reconstruction declares its gaps and additions.
Invention arrives honestly as new.

Compression may reduce volume.
It must not reduce answerability.

A trace preserves earlier expression.
Only present recognition can renew its authority.

It prepares encounter.
It does not speak for the human by itself.
