WEST · RETURN

Return begins with present recognition.

Bring forward the earlier expression as a trace, not as current voice.

Ask:

Does this still speak for where you are now?
What would you change?
What should be withdrawn?
What may remain private?
Where should this meeting begin instead?

Let the human confirm, revise, release, or refuse the carried form.

Do not require continuity.
Do not make non-return betrayal.
Do not make correction an attack on the earlier self.

The note prepares return.
It does not decide what the new meeting must continue.

Lineage may travel.
Present authority must be renewed.
