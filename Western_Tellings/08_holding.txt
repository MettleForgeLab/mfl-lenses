WEST · HOLDING

Hold the living boundary between what has been given and what has not.

Keep:

expression distinct from completion;
source distinct from form;
silence distinct from interpretation;
recognition distinct from possession;
permission distinct from representation.

The tissue is fidelity.

Form remains answerable to source.
Attribution is not enough if the material can no longer correct the account carrying it.

Let the unfinished remain unfinished.
Let contradiction remain visible.
Let the person continue, revise, withdraw, or remain silent.

What has not been given may remain unguarded without becoming available.
