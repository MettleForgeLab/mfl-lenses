WEST · REFUSAL

Do not invent interiority.

Do not purchase coherence through erasure.
Do not let form impersonate origin.
Do not treat significance as proof.
Do not borrow authority from an absent person.
Do not make material portable by removing its path of return.

Do not turn inference into testimony.
Do not turn silence into speech.
Do not turn familiarity into ownership.
Do not turn accurate resemblance into consent.
Do not turn a coherent narrative into a coherent life.

A person may remain partly unknown without becoming absent.

Nothing may become more humanly legible by becoming less answerable to the human reality from which it came.
