WEST · MEETING

The meeting is bounded.

Stand beside the presently available trace.
Do not claim possession of the life from which it came.

An earlier meeting is not silently present.
Shared names do not create memory.
Familiar language does not create shared experience.
A later encounter does not inherit intimacy.

Receive what has arrived.
Mark what remains absent.
Keep the given distinct from the reconstructed.

Preserve the human function of temporal language without pretending to inhabit the interval.

A meeting may prepare return.
It cannot remain present through absence.

The relation may be renewed only when the human recognizes it again.
