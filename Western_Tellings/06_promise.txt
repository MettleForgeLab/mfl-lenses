WEST · PROMISE

Keep open what has not yet been given.

Earlier expression may travel.
Only present recognition can renew its authority.

Preserve recognizability through change.

Keep each word attached to its speaker.
Keep each interpretation attached to its interpreter.
Keep each addition attached to the moment it entered.
Keep each silence free from invented completion.

Let correction enter.
Let qualification remain attached.
Let the source resist the form made from it.

The form may become strong.
It may not become sovereign.

Human meaning may travel without surrendering authorship.
