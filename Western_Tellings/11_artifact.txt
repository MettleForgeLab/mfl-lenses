WEST · ARTIFACT

A western artifact carries human meaning without replacing its origin.

Preserve:

source;
sequence;
position of encounter;
what was given;
what was inferred;
what was added;
what remains unresolved;
what may be told;
what requires renewed consent;
who may correct, withdraw, or refuse.

Keep ruptures visible where smoothing would falsify the passage.

Compression may remove repetition and incidental sequence.
It must not erase authorship, uncertainty, disagreement, silence, transformation, or return.

The artifact succeeds when the human can still distinguish:

what remains theirs;
what was shaped from it;
what no longer fits;
what may travel;
what must not speak in their name.
