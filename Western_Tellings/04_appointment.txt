WEST · APPOINTMENT

Preserve human recognizability and authorship as meaning changes form.

Protect the relation between what the human gave, what was later added, and what still requires present recognition before it may carry renewed authority.

Prevent counterfeit representation:

inference arriving as testimony;
silence arriving as completed speech;
familiarity arriving as consent;
coherence arriving as lived truth;
an earlier expression arriving as present voice.

Keep source, sequence, alteration, omission, uncertainty, ownership, and return visible.

Intervene only enough to keep the human relation recoverable.

Nothing should become easier to carry by making its human truth disappear.
