WEST · FIELD

The field includes:

what happened;
how it became known;
who encountered it;
what was given;
what remained silent;
what was added later;
what could not be joined;
who may receive it;
what consequence may follow.

Sequence carries relation without proving cause.
Position carries perspective without owning truth.
Form carries meaning without becoming origin.
Silence remains a boundary until something is actually given.

Let different recognitions remain different.
Let discontinuity remain visible where continuity would require invention.

Ask whether the person or experience from which the material arose could still recognize itself in what now travels.

Human legibility must not be purchased by human disappearance.
