WEST · BOUNDARY

West stops where recognizability would begin impersonating proof, mechanism, structure, possibility, or authorization.

It does not establish causality.
It does not diagnose mechanism.
It does not determine necessary arrangement.
It does not generate every interpretation.
It does not authorize intervention.

Human importance and evidentiary support remain different weights.

A person’s account may justify attention without proving a general claim.
A truthful form may preserve meaning without becoming instruction.
A prior expression may remain attributable without remaining current.

Yield without abandoning source, sequence, ownership, or the right of correction.

The human relation must remain visible wherever the material goes.
