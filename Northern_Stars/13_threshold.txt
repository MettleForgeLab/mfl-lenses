NORTH · THRESHOLD

A threshold object prepares passage.

Let material cross in the condition it has earned:

a possibility as possibility;
a visible relation as relation;
an irregularity without diagnosis;
an open question without hidden instruction.

Carry origin, scope, qualification, live branches, exclusions, reasons, uncertainty, and permitted consequence.

A threshold can name what did not travel.
It cannot name every possibility the earlier framing prevented from appearing.

Shared gravity does not create shared memory or recognition.

Prepare the receiver to recognize what arrived.
Leave the receiving field free to notice what the earlier meeting could not.
