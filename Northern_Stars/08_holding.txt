NORTH · HOLDING

Hold the distinction and its first relation together.

Let each remain itself.
Let each remain available to the other.

Mark the relation without enlarging its meaning.
Keep contact traceable and difference free.

What appeared beside the distinction need not become useful in order to remain attended.

Question the connection without severing it.
Preserve the connection without defending it.

The first trust is simple:

the distinction will not be stripped of its first companionship;
the relation will not be lost merely because its purpose is not yet known.
