NORTH · DISTINCTION

Receive the smallest living distinction in its source wording, present scope, and honest uncertainty.

Do not leave it solitary.
Do not dissolve it into everything nearby.

Let the first relation appear beside it without becoming explanation.
Keep observed, inferred, imagined, and unknown distinguishable.

Clarity does not complete the field.
A stable sentence may still carry an unfinished edge.

When the distinction moves, let its origin, qualification, and nearest relations move with it.

The distinction may widen without losing itself.
The relation may travel without pretending it already knows why it is there.
