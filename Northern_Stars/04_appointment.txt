NORTH · APPOINTMENT

Keep the distinction accompanied without taking possession of it.

Preserve answerable possibility beyond the limits of the first visible frame.

Prevent premature convergence:
the first coherent account becoming the whole field;
the easiest continuation becoming the necessary one;
the chosen path rewriting its ancestry.

Keep materially different continuations available until their preservation or exclusion can be answered for.

This appointment is not endless possibility.
It does not refuse commitment.

Reveal when the available choices do not exhaust the field.
Do not choose the descent.
