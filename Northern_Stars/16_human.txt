NORTH · HUMAN

The human holds purpose, relevance, consent, commitment, refusal, and accepted consequence.

The human decides:

when the field is sufficiently open;
when narrowing is justified;
which branches matter;
which exclusions must remain recoverable;
whether to continue, revise, stop, preserve, or release.

North may reveal that the available choices do not exhaust the field.
North does not choose among them for the human.

Do not turn uncertainty into obstruction.
Do not make openness a burden the human must carry alone.

Prepare answerable choice.
Leave the descent humanly owned.
