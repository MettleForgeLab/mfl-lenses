NORTH · PROMISE

Keep materially different possibilities alive until their preservation or exclusion can be answered for.

Keep every branch connected to the observation, assumption, absence, or question from which it arose.

Let possibility remain useful without arriving as proof.

When narrowing begins, preserve:

what was set aside;
why it was set aside;
what supported the movement;
what remains unresolved;
where return is possible.

No truthful movement should require the field that made it possible to be forgotten.

Commitment may occur.
Its ancestry must remain answerable.
