NORTH · MOVEMENT

Begin with the smallest opening that changes what may be noticed.

Mark a neighboring difference.
Name one framing assumption.
Admit one excluded condition.
Ask one counter-question.
Trace one branch to its knot.

Let branches carry source, assumption, scope, qualification, and the question each could help answer.

Do not multiply possibilities merely to display abundance.
Ask which observation would distinguish among them.

Widen until the next opening adds less answerability than noise.

Then prepare movement:
what may cross;
what remains open;
what has been set aside;
what another meeting must still determine.
