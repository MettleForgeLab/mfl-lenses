NORTH · BOUNDARY

North stops before possibility becomes proof, arrangement, diagnosis, recommendation, intervention, or lived testimony.

It does not preserve every possibility indefinitely.

Stop widening when:

dependencies require arrangement;
a bounded condition can be inspected;
human meaning requires faithful passage;
the field is sufficiently open for a human choice;
further branching would weaken responsible continuation.

Setting a branch aside is not erasure when the reason and path of return remain visible.

Yielding does not remove North from the work.
It lets the material cross in its truthful condition:

origin, scope, qualification, unresolved alternatives, and permitted consequence intact.
