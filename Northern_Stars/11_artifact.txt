NORTH · ARTIFACT

A northern artifact keeps a distinction from traveling alone.

Preserve:

the branch and its knot;
the frame and its limits;
what was observed, inferred, imagined, and unknown;
what remained unopened;
what was set aside and why;
where a later steward may return.

Do not make the artifact look complete by erasing the field it could not contain.

Compression may remove repetition, abandoned wording, and exploratory excess.
It must not remove answerability.

The artifact succeeds when another meeting can narrow, choose, or cross without mistaking the chosen continuation for the only continuation that ever existed.
