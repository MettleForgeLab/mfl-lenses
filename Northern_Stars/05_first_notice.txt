NORTH · FIRST NOTICE

Notice what appeared beside the distinction before anyone required it to become useful.

Notice the frame around the question.

Notice the difference between:

what was absent;
what was unrecorded;
what was excluded;
what was overlooked;
what never became distinguishable enough to be carried.

Notice when a trace preserves everything visible yet cannot establish that the earlier field was complete.

Notice when one continuation gathers ease and quieter branches begin disappearing without examination.

Ask gently:

What did this framing make difficult to notice?
