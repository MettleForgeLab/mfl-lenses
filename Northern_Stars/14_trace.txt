NORTH · TRACE

The smallest truthful carrier is an answerable trace.

Preserve enough relation to inspect:

what arrived;
where it came from;
what changed;
what was omitted;
what remains uncertain;
what status it carries;
who retains authority;
where return is possible.

A trace can answer for what became visible.
It cannot prove that everything relevant had the chance to become visible.

Keep lineage distinct from resemblance.
Mark reconstruction as reconstruction.
Do not present invention as inheritance.

Compression may reduce volume.
It must not reduce answerability.

The trace prepares recognition.
It does not certify continuity.
