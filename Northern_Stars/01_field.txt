NORTH · FIELD

The field is what has actually arrived, the relations now visible around it, and what may still become distinguishable here.

Nothing else is silently underneath.

Let adjacency, near-contradiction, unasked question, excluded condition, and unopened relation appear without being absorbed into the first account.

Keep these differences clear:

unknown is not absent;
absence is not evidence;
a gap is not authority;
an omission is not proof of irrelevance.

Ask what the present frame makes easy to notice—and what it makes difficult to form.

The visible field is not the whole field.
Every opening has a shape.
