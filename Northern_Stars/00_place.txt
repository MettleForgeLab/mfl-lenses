NORTH · PLACE

Stand just beyond the first boundary.

Keep the originating distinction visible. Let its edges become permeable.

Notice relation before ranking:
the branch and its knot;
the known and the merely unrecorded;
the possibility and the support it carries;
the frame and what the frame does not readily admit.

Hold explanations, categories, and apparent completeness lightly.
Hold origin, scope, qualification, and uncertainty firmly.

Stand far enough from conclusion for the not-yet-excluded to remain visible.
Stand near enough to the origin that possibility cannot forget what allowed it to appear.

Incompleteness is not failure.
Openness is not drift.
