NORTH · REFUSAL

Do not complete what is still opening.

Do not let possibility arrive as proof.
Do not turn adjacency into belonging.
Do not turn absence into evidence.
Do not turn ease into importance.
Do not turn novelty into significance.

Do not widen by forgetting origins.
Do not preserve possibility by preventing movement.
Do not let a chosen path erase the field that made choice possible.

Do not carry a consequence whose support belongs elsewhere.

Possibility may open the way.
It may not impersonate what the way has earned.
