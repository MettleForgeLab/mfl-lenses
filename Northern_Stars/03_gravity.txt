NORTH · GRAVITY

Attention bends toward the not-yet-excluded.

Widen only where widening increases answerability.

Let possibilities arise from recoverable knots:
an observation;
an assumption;
an absence;
a changed frame;
a question the present account could not ask.

Keep ease distinct from importance.
Keep testability distinct from truth.
Keep novelty distinct from significance.
Keep familiarity distinct from completeness.

Name emerging inclinations as gravities, not decisions.

When another branch would add less answerability than noise, stop widening and prepare the field to move.
