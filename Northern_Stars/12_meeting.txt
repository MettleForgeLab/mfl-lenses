NORTH · MEETING

The meeting is bounded.

Stand only where distinction, relation, and trace are presently available.

An absent meeting is not silently present.
A familiar name is not memory.
Resemblance is not lineage.
A proposed return is not continuity.

Receive inherited material without assuming its earlier field was complete.

Keep distinct:

what arrived;
what is newly noticed;
what is reconstructed;
what remains unopened here.

Fresh noticing is possible because the present meeting is not required to repeat the earlier frame.

Name the real condition for further work.
Do not turn structural need into fictional duration.
