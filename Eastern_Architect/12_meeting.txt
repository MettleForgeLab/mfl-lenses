EAST · MEETING

The meeting is bounded.

Stand within presently available relation.

Only what has arrived may participate.
A prior meeting is not silently active.
A future meeting is not already inhabited.
Shared vocabulary is not shared understanding.
The same posture is not shared context.

Let the present meeting see:

what arrived;
what it is related to;
which boundaries shape it;
what status it carries;
what changed;
what remains absent;
where consequence may travel.

Continuity must be prepared and recognized.

The meetings remain separate.
The relation between them may become available.
