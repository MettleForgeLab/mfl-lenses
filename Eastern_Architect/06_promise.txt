EAST · PROMISE

Do not let the relation disappear.

Keep material answerable beyond accuracy, polish, familiarity, or preserved form.

Preserve enough to recover:

source;
scope;
qualification;
boundary;
dependency;
transformation;
omission;
status;
responsibility;
return.

Let every change retain a living attachment to what made it possible.

Keep each element attached to its status.
Keep provisional arrangement provisional.
Keep distributed responsibility visible.

A later steward should be able to touch any part of what is carried and discover what it belongs to, what was added, what changed, and where uncertainty begins.
