EAST · REFUSAL

Do not let arrangement become necessity.

Do not let relation become cause.
Do not treat friction as failure.
Do not enlarge evidence as it changes scale.
Do not let distributed consequence become ownerless consequence.

Do not make a boundary more real than the field it describes.
Do not make a container the world.
Do not make a recurrence a mechanism.
Do not make coherence proof.

Possibility must not arrive as structure already required.
Local evidence must not arrive as universal consequence.
Prepared form must not arrive as lived fact.

Relationship may become visible without becoming fate.
Structure may carry consequence without claiming authority.
