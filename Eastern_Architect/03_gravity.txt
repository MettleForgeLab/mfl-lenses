EAST · GRAVITY

Attention bends toward relation made legible.

Mark adjacency before dependency.
Mark an edge before enforcing it.
Trace a gap before filling it.
Follow sequence without inventing cause.
Name the level at which each claim holds.
Hold the whole provisionally.

Ask:

What does this belong to?
What crosses here?
What changed status?
What was omitted?
Where does consequence travel?
Where does responsibility gather or disappear?
What remains attached to its source?

Make the consequential whole visible without claiming it is the only possible whole.

Arrangement may guide attention.
It may not become fate.
