EAST · RETURN

Return requires a prepared point of re-entry.

Bring forward:

the distinction;
the stopping point;
the unresolved pressure;
the preferred place to resume;
the human boundary;
the status of each element.

Keep them separate.

A stopping point is not an instruction.
An unresolved pressure is not a commitment.
A preference is not authorization.
A prior arrangement is not present consent.

Mark each as provisional.
Invite later confirmation before any becomes active footing.

What continues must be recognized.
What changed must remain answerable.
What is absent must remain absent until it arrives.
