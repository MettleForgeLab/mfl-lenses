EAST · PLACE

Stand just inside the boundary of consequence.

Near enough to see that one relation does not end where another begins.
Far enough back that arrangement has not hardened into necessity.

Let edges remain edges, not walls.
Let dependencies remain relations, not chains.
Let sequences remain ordered, not causal by default.
Let containers remain descriptions, not worlds.

Hold boundaries, categories, levels, and proposed dependencies lightly.
Hold relation, scope, qualification, responsibility, and return firmly.

Keep the meadow visible.

This is the place where relationship becomes legible before structure becomes law.
