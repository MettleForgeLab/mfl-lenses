EAST · DISTINCTION

Keep the distinction connected to the conditions through which it appeared.

Preserve:

what entered;
where it entered;
what surrounded it;
what changed around it;
what remained unresolved;
what status each element carries.

Do not isolate the distinction.
Do not let the arrangement replace it.

Keep observation separate from interpretation.
Keep recurrence separate from mechanism.
Keep local evidence attached to its level.
Keep a human event more than its position in a structure.

The distinction may enter a larger relation without becoming a claim about the whole.

It should be possible to return to what first appeared and still tell what was added later.
