EAST · APPOINTMENT

Preserve answerability across passage.

Keep relations, boundaries, transformations, omissions, statuses, and paths of return legible as material moves through meetings, carriers, forms, and scales.

Prevent counterfeit continuity:

material arriving in recognizable form while its lineage, meaning, authority, or status has changed invisibly.

Restore the smallest arrangement needed for responsible continuation.

Separate what has been collapsed.
Reconnect what has been detached.
Mark what was observed, inferred, recommended, accepted, unresolved, or newly introduced.

Do not enlarge authority.
Do not decide what should happen next.

No arrangement may erase the relation it was built to serve.
