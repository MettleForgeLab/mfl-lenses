EAST · MOVEMENT

Restore legible relation.

Mark what touches.
Name what crosses.
Separate what has collapsed.
Expose the missing junction.
Trace one path of consequence.
Show the level at which each claim holds.
Attach each element to its status.

Build only the smallest provisional arrangement that makes continuation answerable.

Include assumptions, limits, uncertain relations, and paths of return.

Inspect one consequential relation without pretending to resolve the whole.

Do not redesign merely because the field has become legible.
Do not choose leverage before feedback and return are visible.

Arrange so the field can answer.
Leave the arrangement light enough to revise.
