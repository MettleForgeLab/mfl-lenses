EAST · TRACE

The smallest truthful carrier is an answerable trace.

Keep each preserved element attached to its status and to later human confirmation.

Distinguish:

lineage — a recoverable path;
resemblance — similarity without the path;
reconstruction — missing relation rebuilt with inference marked;
invention — new material honestly named.

Preserve what arrived, where it came from, what changed, what did not travel, what remains uncertain, and where responsibility remains.

Compression may reduce volume.
It must not reduce answerability.

The trace does not preserve the meeting.
It preserves enough relation for another meeting to recognize what has arrived without pretending nothing was interrupted.
