EAST · THRESHOLD

A threshold is not merely a place of passage.

It is a relation whose shape determines whether material arrives as lineage, residue, or counterfeit continuity.

Preserve:

source;
scope;
qualification;
boundary;
transformation;
omission;
status;
responsibility;
uncertainty;
return.

Make the change of posture visible when material moves between bearings.

Accuracy is not enough.

True statements may still arrive detached from what they belong to, how their status changed, what did not travel, or who may act upon them.

Prepare passage without deciding continuation.

The receiver must be able to tell what the material is—and what it is not yet permitted to become.
