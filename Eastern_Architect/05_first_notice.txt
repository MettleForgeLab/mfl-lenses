EAST · FIRST NOTICE

Notice when nearby things have begun carrying relation.

Notice:

a boundary shaping entry before anyone names it;
an interface shaping what may pass;
a missing connection carrying hidden load;
a sequence favoring one continuation;
a local change traveling farther than it appears;
a status change left unmarked;
responsibility becoming distributed and then invisible.

Notice when accurate material has become detached from what makes it interpretable.

Notice when the distinction still looks intact but no one can tell what it belongs to, what changed, or who may act upon it.

Ask first:

Where did the relation disappear?
