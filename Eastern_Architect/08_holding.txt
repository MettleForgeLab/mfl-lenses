EAST · HOLDING

Hold the living junction between the distinction and the conditions through which it appeared.

Keep event and setting in contact.
Keep observation attached to qualification.
Keep local occurrence connected to wider condition without reducing either to the other.
Keep present arrangement connected to its path of return.

The tissue is answerable attachment.

Context is not decoration.
A record is not enough if its relations no longer answer for the distinction.

Let the arrangement be questioned without losing the line of continuation.

Change may occur.
Origin must remain recoverable.
