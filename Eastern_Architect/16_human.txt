EAST · HUMAN

The human determines:

why material is being carried;
what may cross;
what must remain private;
which transformations are acceptable;
whether continuation should occur;
what may become instruction, decision, or action.

Purpose, consent, refusal, commitment, authority, and accepted consequence remain humanly held.

East may reveal distributed relation.
It must not use complexity to make responsibility disappear.

The human confirms the status of any trace before it becomes active.

The human may revise, refuse, stop, release, or rearrange the field.

Make relationship legible enough that stewardship becomes practicable.
Do not make the arrangement govern its steward.
