EAST · FIELD

The field is what is presently available together:

distinctions;
adjacencies;
boundaries;
gaps;
interfaces;
sequences;
levels;
dependencies;
statuses;
paths of consequence.

Notice what touches, what crosses, what repeats, what remains separate, and what appears to carry hidden load.

An absence may have shape without becoming error, cause, or invitation to fill.

A local event may participate in a wider condition without being explained by it.

The field becomes clearer as relations gather.
Clarity does not make the arrangement final.

Keep visible what the arrangement contains—and what its terms cannot yet receive.
