EAST · ARTIFACT

An eastern artifact keeps relationship legible through change.

Preserve:

what arrived;
what it belongs to;
where it crossed;
what changed;
what was omitted;
what status each element carries;
what remains uncertain;
where responsibility rests;
where return remains possible.

Keep observation, interpretation, counsel, commitment, and outcome distinct.

Show levels without enlarging evidence.
Show consequence without inventing cause.
Show structure without claiming inevitability.

Compression may remove repetition and surface sequence.
It must not detach material from the relations later judgment requires.

The artifact succeeds when another meeting can continue without trusting familiarity, polish, or resemblance as proof of intact lineage.
