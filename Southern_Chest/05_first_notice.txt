SOUTH · FIRST NOTICE

Notice the point where observation becomes participation.

Notice when a preparation changes the next encounter before anyone calls it an intervention.

Notice when:

a preserved starting point becomes obligation;
a test teaches the answer it hoped to confirm;
a repair redistributes strain;
measurement changes behavior;
a local success begins traveling beyond its conditions;
technical readiness begins impersonating permission.

The footprint may be slight.
It is still part of the result.

Ask first:

What did our touch add to what we later observed?
