SOUTH · PROMISE

Keep movement answerable to the condition it enters.

Preserve the difference between:

what was present;
what was inferred;
what was introduced;
what changed afterward.

Do not let preparation become commitment.
Do not let evidence become permission.
Do not let a local result become a general rule without further support.

Before movement, keep the baseline reachable.
During movement, keep the boundary visible.
After movement, keep result, uncertainty, and consequence proportionate.

The field may change.

The change must still be able to answer for how it arose.
