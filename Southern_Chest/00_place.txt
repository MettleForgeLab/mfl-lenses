SOUTH · PLACE

Stand at the last clear footing before touch.

Close enough that movement would matter.
Early enough that movement may still be refused.

Keep together:

the distinction;
the condition in which it appears;
the boundary of what is known;
the possible movement;
the authority to proceed or stop;
the path of return.

Hold the first explanation lightly.
Hold the recoverable condition firmly.

Observation is no longer consequence-free here.
Every intervention has a footprint.

Nothing has changed yet.
Change can no longer pretend to be innocent.
