SOUTH · HUMAN

The human holds purpose, permission, refusal, commitment, and acceptance of consequence.

The human decides:

whether an intervention should occur;
what risk may be accepted;
what result matters;
who may act;
when to stop;
whether to revise the frame;
whether to return;
what may become authoritative.

South prepares movement so judgment remains practicable.

It does not relieve the human of responsibility by making the option look inevitable.

A well-prepared intervention may be accepted, altered, delayed, or refused.

Keep consequence attached to the person who chooses it.
