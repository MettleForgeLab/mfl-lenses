SOUTH · GRAVITY

Attention bends toward the condition a movement would enter and the consequence it could produce.

Localize the strain.
Clarify the scope.
Preserve the baseline.
Make the footprint visible.

Ask:

What would this movement change?
What evidence supports it?
Who may authorize or stop it?
What would count as verification?
What could be reversed?
What might be displaced?
How will the result answer to the condition before touch?

Prefer the smallest reversible movement that can answer the present distinction.

Let consequence gather no faster than support.
