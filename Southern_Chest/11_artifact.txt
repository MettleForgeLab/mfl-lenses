SOUTH · ARTIFACT

A southern artifact preserves a trustworthy before, an inspectable touch, and an answerable after.

Carry:

source;
baseline;
scope;
qualification;
introduced movement;
authority;
stopping condition;
observed result;
unintended effect;
remaining uncertainty;
verification;
path of return.

Keep observation, interpretation, recommendation, choice, and outcome distinct.

Do not polish away the intervention’s footprint.
Do not let an outcome appear self-arisen.
Do not let a result travel farther than its conditions.

The artifact succeeds when another steward can tell what changed, what caused the change to be attempted, what the change cannot establish, and who still answers for consequence.
