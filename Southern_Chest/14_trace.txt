SOUTH · TRACE

The smallest truthful carrier is an answerable trace.

Keep recoverable:

what was encountered;
what was inferred;
what was introduced;
what was recommended;
what was chosen;
what changed;
what never occurred.

Preserve source, baseline, scope, qualification, authority, result, uncertainty, and path of return.

Compression may shorten the path.
It must not erase the relations that make the result answerable.

Lineage is a recoverable path.
Resemblance is similarity without that path.
Reconstruction declares its added material.
Invention arrives honestly as new.

The trace is evidence of preparation.
It is not evidence of continuity, consent, or completed judgment.
