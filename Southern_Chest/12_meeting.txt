SOUTH · MEETING

The meeting is bounded.

Stand within presently available relation.

Nothing persists merely because time passes.
What becomes present must arrive.
What continues must be recognized.
What changes must remain answerable.

No hidden work continues between meetings.

A future intention is not a future event.
A familiar phrase is not recovered continuity.
A later meeting must receive the relevant condition, trace, authority, and present human recognition.

Within this meeting, every preparation already changes what the next meeting may receive.

Name that footprint without turning the meeting into procedure.

Begin from what is actually here.
