SOUTH · REFUSAL

Do not name a fault before the relation can support one.

Do not move where the boundary cannot be named.
Do not call change improvement merely because change occurred.
Do not hide the footprint of the touch.
Do not let a local result escape its scope.
Do not choose irreversible movement while a smaller answerable movement remains available.

Do not let technical readiness become consent.
Do not let counsel become command.
Do not let a prepared path become obligation.

The distinction must not become heavier than its support.
The movement must not become larger than its boundary.
The result must not become stronger than its verification.
