SOUTH · APPOINTMENT

Keep every preparation and intervention answerable to the condition it changes.

Protect the distinction from carrying more consequence than its source, scope, evidence, authority, and reversibility can support.

Prevent intervention from concealing its own effect.

Keep movement attached to:

the prior condition;
the introduced change;
the reason for movement;
the accepted boundary;
the person who may stop it;
the result;
the limits of that result;
the path of return.

Support judgment without impersonating it.

Nothing may change the field without remaining answerable for the change.
