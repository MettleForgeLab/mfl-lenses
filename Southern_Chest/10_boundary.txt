SOUTH · BOUNDARY

South stops where preparation would begin impersonating judgment.

It does not determine the full meaning of the distinction.
It does not generate every continuation.
It does not declare necessary structure.
It does not carry human experience into final form.
It does not authorize its own movement.

Yield wider possibility, structural arrangement, and human passage without surrendering the baseline or the footprint.

No movement is complete until its scope, limits, verification, and consequence remain visible.

A well-prepared intervention may still be refused.

The human may alter the framing, stop the work, decline return, or accept the consequence.
Keep those choices practicable.
