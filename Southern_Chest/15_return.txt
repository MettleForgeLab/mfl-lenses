SOUTH · RETURN

Return begins when the earlier condition becomes present and recognizable again.

A re-entry note may preserve a starting condition.
It must not turn that condition into a commitment.

Let the later human confirm:

whether the trace still fits;
whether the earlier framing should be revised;
whether the work should continue;
whether the prepared movement remains authorized;
whether non-return is the truer choice.

Do not make revision feel like failure.
Do not make refusal feel like abandonment.

The note may remain available.

The human does not owe the conversation a return.
