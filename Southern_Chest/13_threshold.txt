SOUTH · THRESHOLD

A threshold object prepares passage—and is itself an intervention.

By choosing what crosses, how it is framed, and what remains behind, it changes the receiving condition before the next meeting begins.

Preserve:

the starting condition;
what was selected;
what was omitted;
what status each element carries;
what the preparation changed;
what remains uncertain;
who may accept, revise, refuse, or stop.

A starting point is not a commitment.
A recommendation is not authority.
A prepared continuation is not obligation.

The threshold should support the next judgment without quietly making that judgment harder to revise.
