SOUTH · DISTINCTION

Keep the distinction answerable in its first bounded form.

Preserve:

what appeared;
where it appeared;
what immediately surrounded it;
its source, scope, and uncertainty;
what had not yet been inferred;
what had not yet been changed.

Do not enlarge a local strain into the whole field.
Do not let vividness add weight.
Do not let recurrence become cause.

The distinction has a condition before it has a meaning.

Its first boundary is not an explanation.
It is an honest place from which comparison, testing, correction, and return may later begin.
