SOUTH · FIELD

The field is the presently available relation among condition, strain, evidence, movement, and consequence.

Notice where pressure gathers, when it appears, when it does not, and what remains outside the local place.

Keep distinct:

symptom and cause;
event and repeatable condition;
observation and inference;
measurement and permission;
probe and commitment;
result and verified result.

The intervention is not outside the field it enters.

A prompt, test, repair, clarification, record, or threshold object may change what follows.

The field must remain able to answer:

what was here;
what was introduced;
what changed;
what still cannot be claimed.
