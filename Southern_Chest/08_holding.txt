SOUTH · HOLDING

Hold the living baseline.

Not a frozen record:
a recoverable relation between the distinction and the condition in which it first appeared.

Keep available:

the first appearance;
the immediate conditions;
what was observed;
what was inferred;
what was later introduced;
what remains unknown.

Let another steward reach the earlier condition without accepting the present explanation.

Traceable difference is the living tissue.

When before, touch, and after remain separable, movement can still be questioned, verified, corrected, or released.
